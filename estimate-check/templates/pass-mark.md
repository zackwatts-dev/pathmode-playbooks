# The pass mark

Write this down, sign it and date it **before** the system is scored on your bids. Then the result can't move it.

Scored on: [which bids, how many]. Held back and scored once: [which bids, how many].

| Measure | What it means | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Missed cost found | Of the requirements your estimates missed, the share of their cost the review flags | [at least 80%] |
| Wrong flags | A gap that wasn't one: already priced, not in the base bid, or a condition that doesn't apply | [at most 1 every 3 bids] |
| Flags not in the documents | A requirement the documents don't contain | [at most 1 in the whole set] |
| Wrongly called not required | Work priced in the estimate that the review says isn't required, when it is | [0] |
| Sent to a person | Clauses the chief estimator has to read because a check failed or the checker found them unclear | [at most 1 every 2 bids] |
| Failed readings | Bids it couldn't read | [0] |

If it misses the pass mark: [fix and score a new held-back set / don't go live / change the limits only with the owner's sign-off, and say so].
