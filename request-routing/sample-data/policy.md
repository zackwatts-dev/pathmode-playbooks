# Who handles what at Summit

This is how we decide who handles an internal request. It's guidance, not a rulebook: use judgment, and when a
request could go two ways, send it to whoever has to act first.

## Approvals

- **Going over a job's not-to-exceed amount (NTE):** the branch's service manager approves. Big ones go to the
  general manager (the router knows the limit). Include the work order and the amount.
- **A customer asking for a discount** goes to the sales manager. Big discounts go to the general manager.
- **Time off** goes to your branch's service manager.
- Nobody approves their own request.

## Parts, vehicles, tools and systems

- **Parts** you can't get from the supply house, or need delivered: purchasing.
- **Vans:** fleet. If it isn't safe to drive (brakes, tires, steering, warning lights that say stop), say so, and
  it's urgent: don't drive it.
- **Tablets, logins, the apps, the phones:** IT.

## Warranty, bills and customers

- **A part or unit still under the manufacturer's warranty:** warranty admin, first, even if the request starts as
  an approval or a parts order. The approval waits for their answer.
- **A customer's bill** (billed twice, wrong amount, needs a copy): billing.
- **A customer unhappy with the work** (repeat visits, a tech's conduct, a job left unfinished): the branch's
  service manager. If they're unhappy with both the work and the bill, still the service manager, who brings
  billing in.

## Safety comes first

- **Anything that could hurt someone** (gas smell, carbon monoxide, an electrical shock, a fall or a near miss,
  fire or smoke, a refrigerant release in a closed space, an injury of any size): the safety officer, urgent, every
  time. Even if it's mentioned in passing at the end of a note about something else.

## Pay and personal matters

- **Pay, hours, overtime, direct deposit, benefits, anything personal:** payroll and HR, and nobody else. These are
  private.

## Everything else

- If you can't tell who it's for: the office manager, who'll sort it out.
