# Emergency service

## When to call the emergency line
Call 480-555-0100 any time, day or night, if you have no cooling and the indoor temperature is above 85°F, a gas smell, water leaking onto equipment or electrical panels, or a unit that is smoking or sparking. If you smell gas, leave the building first and call your gas utility or 911.

## Response times
For customers with a service agreement, we aim to arrive within 4 hours for emergencies in the Phoenix metro area. Without an agreement, we arrive as soon as a technician is free, usually the same day.

## After-hours charges
Emergency visits between 5pm and 7am, on weekends and on holidays are billed at the after-hours labor rate. Service agreement customers pay the standard rate for emergencies.
