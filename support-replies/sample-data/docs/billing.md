# Billing and payments

## Payment terms
Invoices are due 30 days from the invoice date unless your contract says otherwise.

## How to pay
You can pay by ACH, check or card through the payment link on your invoice. We never ask customers to change payment details by email; if you get such an email claiming to be from us, call us at 480-555-0100.

## Questions about an invoice
Reply to the invoice email or call billing at 480-555-0102. Include the invoice number. We'll review the technician's time records and respond within two business days.
