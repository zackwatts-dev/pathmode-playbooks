# Warranty

## Our labor warranty
We warranty our repair labor for 90 days. If the same repair fails within 90 days, we return at no labor charge.

## Manufacturer parts warranty
Parts carry the manufacturer's warranty, usually 1 year for repair parts and 5 to 10 years for new equipment that's registered. We handle the warranty claim, but labor to replace a warranty part after our 90 days is billed.

## New equipment installations
New equipment we install includes a 1-year labor warranty from the installation date.
