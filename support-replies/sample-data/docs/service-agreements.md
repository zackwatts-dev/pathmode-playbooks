# Service agreements

## What's included
A service agreement includes two preventive maintenance visits a year (spring cooling check and fall heating check), filter changes at each visit, priority emergency response, and the standard labor rate for emergencies at any hour.

## What's not included
Parts and refrigerant are billed separately. Repairs found during a maintenance visit are quoted before any work is done.

## Cancelling
You can cancel a service agreement with 30 days' written notice. Visits already completed are not refunded.
