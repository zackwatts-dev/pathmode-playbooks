# Service Technician (Commercial HVAC), Chandler

## Must have
- EPA Section 608 certification, Universal or Type II
- At least 2 years of hands-on commercial HVAC service experience
- A valid driver's license
- Able to work on rooftops and ladders, with or without reasonable accommodation

## Nice to have
- NATE certification
- Experience with building automation or controls
- Experience with rooftop units over 10 tons
